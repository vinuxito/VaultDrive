## Whole-system effect

Completed accepted outcomes; manual steering; mandatory approvals; recovery; neighbor
burden; resources; maintenance; adoption and exit. State numerator, denominator, workload,
comparison limits, and which evidence supports each claim.

## Risks and authority

Open blockers; enforced stop or merely requested hold; current allowed actions; decision
owner. Keep historical strengths separate from current release readiness.

## Smallest next move

Hypothesis; owner; exact change or observation; permitted scope; cost/time bounds;
acceptance; rollback/compensation; rejected simpler alternative; remaining uncertainty.
