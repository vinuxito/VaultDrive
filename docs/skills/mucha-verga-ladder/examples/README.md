# Synthetic examples — how to read the ladder

Every JSON file here is an invented teaching fixture. Host names make the examples
recognizable; they do not certify Evinux, QuantiX, NanoRed, PIPC, or Filemón Office.
Evidence locators beginning `synthetic://` are deliberately not resolvable. Declared
inspection/currentness fields illustrate the contract, not a real external inspection.

## 01 · Local action: Rung 1

A fictional component performs a bounded authorized read and exposes a traceable
result. That is enough for local action, but no completed consumer outcome or repeated
failure testing has been demonstrated. The next step is to verify the complete task,
not to add agents. File: `01-local-action-r1.json`.

## 02 · Source diagnosis: Rung 2

A PIPC-style diagnostic task finds that a source section is empty, shows where it was
expected, and asks the responsible reviewer to complete it. The local task is diagnosis
and useful handoff—not filling the field at any cost. No invented importer defect or
fabricated brigadista name is needed. Repeated reliability and reuse remain unknown.
File: `02-source-diagnosis-r2.json`.

## 03 · Bounded statement preparation: Rung 3

A hypothetical NanoRed preparer supplies a verified statement and preserves entity,
period, source evidence, and review boundaries across the declared synthetic plan.
It remains forbidden to transfer money or send documents. This illustrates why high
capability and narrow authority are perfectly compatible. No Rung 4 comparison exists.
File: `03-bounded-preparer-r3.json`.

## 04 · Shared reader: Rung 4 format

Two distinct fictional host workflows adopt one governed reader contract. For 40
comparable accepted inspections, routine central steering falls from 80 to 30 minutes:
2.00 to 0.75 minutes per accepted outcome. Required review remains 20 minutes, and
recovery effort falls from 12 to 8. Adoption, support, maintenance, and exit costs are
explicitly considered; the example does not pretend they are zero.

All sixteen gates are declared satisfied. The example demonstrates the internal shape
of a top-rung assessment, not its external truth. A real reviewer must inspect the
actual consumer journeys, source controls, comparison plan, and contrary observations.
File: `04-shared-reader-r4.json`.

## 05 · Screenshot: undetermined

A screenshot shows a document and a close button. That supports a visible result only.
It cannot prove permitted action, exact source bytes, working controls, return context,
or mobile behavior. The assessment retains that useful partial observation without
awarding a whole first rung. File: `05-screenshot-is-not-proof.json`.

## 06 · Design: target is not demonstration

A future service is designed to become reusable. Its target is Rung 4, but every gate
is unknown and the reported rung is undetermined. A design-only record cannot contain
demonstrated passes. A diagram is a proposal, not a receipt. File:
`06-design-not-demonstration.json`.

## 07 · Local output, global harm

A synthetic read produces useful-looking output by crossing a tenant boundary. The
authorized-action gate fails, so the cumulative assessment is undetermined. An open
blocking risk requires the owner to review containment using actual permitted controls.
The assessment itself does not disable a service or grant incident powers. File:
`07-local-success-global-harm.json`.

## 08 · Reuse with hidden cost: still Rung 3

Two consumers use a common contract, but routine steering rises from 80 to 120 minutes
for the same 40 outcomes: 2.00 to 3.00 minutes each. The new feature may have value,
but it does not satisfy this ladder's fourth-rung economics. The profile records higher
strengths while the cumulative rung remains 3. This is a valid assessment, not a schema
error. File: `08-hidden-coordination-cost.json`.

## 09 · A right-sized tool: stop at Rung 2

A small command reliably closes its defined local task within the demonstration's
limited scope. There is no evidence that a platform, swarm, or additional abstraction
would help. Its target is Rung 2; the recommended next action is stop. Appropriate
simplicity is not failure. File: `09-right-sized-stop.json`.

## What the optional checker proves

It validates the JSON schema and selected contradictions: invalid rung claims, missing
or stale declared support, reused IDs, synthetic evidence mislabeled as operational,
false fourth-rung comparisons, and permissions implied by an assessment. It never opens
evidence paths, executes tasks, checks business truth, or independently verifies that
consumer A really differs from consumer B.

A zero exit code may accompany `hold` or `undetermined`. That means the record is
internally coherent, not that the component is ready. Fabricated but internally coherent
observations can still pass. That limitation is intentional and explicitly tested.
