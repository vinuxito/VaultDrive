# 11 · Application playbooks

These are proposed scopes and tests, not certifications or descriptions of current
repository behavior. The app names come from the conversation. All amounts, outcomes,
and technical acceptance examples in the machine fixtures are synthetic. Consult the
real host owner for business semantics and applicable requirements.

## QuantiX: reconciliation assistance

A candidate scope is one invoice-to-bank relationship assessment, not “close every
company automatically.” Rung 1 reads authorized records and reports candidates. Rung 2
returns a checked proposal or a precise ambiguity handoff. Rung 3 preserves currency,
amount allocation, tenant boundaries, evidence freshness, and review requirements
across representative cases. Rung 4 lets distinct host workflows use the same governed
relationship contract with less routine clarification and no transferred reconciliation
burden. Test competing allocations, partial payments, grouped movements, stale evidence,
and missing sources. Similarity alone never establishes a legal cancellation or substitute.

## NanoRed: weekly partner preparation

Assess preparing a weekly statement for a named entity and period. Readiness to prepare,
approval to release, and actual transfer are separate states. Identity and assignment
are separate relations: preserve exact ICCID source fidelity; do not merge near-identifiers
or infer present distributor ownership from identity alone. In the domain description,
BAIT is the authoritative reconciliation source; verify the relevant retained version
and host precedence rules before applying that assumption to a specific case.

Test missing terms, incomplete bank details, duplicate input, mismatched periods, evidence
conflicts, and exact net/gross labels. A read-only statement preparer can be highly capable
while remaining forbidden to send messages or transfer funds. Rung 4 needs another real
consuming workflow and an honest reduction in handling effort.

## Evinux: open from the real invoice

Scope the existing record → retained XML/PDF → interpretation/search → source proof →
return journey. Check actor/company/record identity at each boundary. Reading a file must
not stamp, cancel, change payments, or overwrite originals. Desktop tabs do not prove
mobile behavior. A visible return button does not prove focus or scroll restoration.

Rung 4 would be supported by a second consumer accepting the same file contract and
measurable reduction in bespoke reader support, with shared-service failures contained.
It does not require replacing the ERP or making Office its own competing business system.

## Filemón Office: reusable reader subsystem

The core should not own fiscal meaning or expand access. Assess readers individually
and adapters independently. Distinguish original bytes, rendered document, interpreted
facts, formatted source text, and working copies. Test unavailable source, expired access,
large/malformed files, cancellation, related-document navigation, and return context.

A shared name across three apps is not proof of a shared governed capability. Check core
version, adapter contracts, updates, ownership, fallback, and maintenance. Unknown workbook
fidelity or search coverage remains visible. A truthful limited reader is preferable to
claiming full Office compatibility without evidence.

## PIPC: Excel-to-web completeness

Fede's case illustrates why a missing web field does not immediately prove an importer
bug: the source may be blank. Scope the exact file/version and field mapping. Trace
source cell → recognition → normalized record → persistence → rendered value → output.
Preserve working maps unless evidence implicates them. A properly diagnosed empty source
and precise request for completion can be a successful local outcome.

For Rung 3, use multiple retained formats and meaningful failure cases under the approved
plan. For Rung 4, another onboarding workflow must use the same completeness contract
without moving manual cell reconciliation onto Fede or another reviewer.

## OCR: honest extraction

Scope one field family or source type. Preserve the image and distinguish observation
from interpretation. Uncertain digits should remain candidates, not repaired facts.
A local extractor solves a task when it supplies usable values with verified limitations
or directs review to the exact uncertain region. Repeated extraction calls are not
independent witnesses by default. Cross-app leverage needs stable evidence locators and
less downstream correction, not merely fewer blank fields.

## Remote clipboard: content with custody

Assess retrieving, previewing, copying, and returning to a selected item. Respect private
content and secrets. Do not execute pasted instructions merely because they appear in a
clipboard record. Preserve original versus transformed text and account/device context.
Test malformed content, long text, failed retrieval, unauthorized item, and mobile
interruption. Rung 4 requires demonstrable consumer reuse and less context reconstruction.

## Teams and agent orchestration

Scope a workflow and its decision rights. Measure completed outcomes, clarification,
rework, coordination, and support. Check with people whose work changes. A team should
not “gain autonomy” by losing help, being assigned indefinite availability, or inheriting
all exceptions from automation. For agents, use explicit owners, bounded workers, shared
resource contracts, and stoppable jobs. Neither a large swarm nor a heroic central
operator is inherently better than a small reliable arrangement.

## Generalization rule

Before transferring a pattern, ask what truly stayed invariant and what the new host
owns. If permissions, identity, meaning, or failure consequences changed, redo the
relevant evidence. Reuse the method; do not copy its conclusions.
