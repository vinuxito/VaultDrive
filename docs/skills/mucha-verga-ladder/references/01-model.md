# 01 · What the ladder means

## The actual unit of assessment

Assess a capability performed by a component under a defined operating envelope.
“Evinux is Rung 4” is too broad to be useful. “The retained-document reader in build X
can serve two independently owned host workflows under scope Y” is testable. State
input types, authority, environment, workload, failure modes, and time window.

A component can have different profiles simultaneously: Rung 3 PDF reading, Rung 1
experimental workbook opening, and unknown mobile recovery. Keep those profiles
separate. Do not average them into a flattering application score.

The four rungs are cumulative within that scope. This is a deliberate assessment
convention, not a claim that all real systems develop in this order. A module can
possess a reusable interface before it has demonstrated reliability. Record that
strength, but do not award the top rung while a foundational gate remains unproven.

## Four distinctions that prevent confusion

Local action means the component can carry out a bounded authorized operation and
report an observation. Local solving means it closes a defined outcome, including
relevant exceptions or a valid blocked handoff, rather than merely issuing commands.
Reliable cooperation adds repeated evidence and preserved system constraints.
System leverage adds useful capability for others without an increase in routine
central steering at comparable workload, while exposing total costs and retaining
necessary oversight.

Nothing in these definitions requires an LLM. A deterministic parser, a well-designed
API, an explicit stop condition, or a clearer human handoff can improve the profile.
“Smart” language and independent planning are neither necessary nor sufficient.

## Capability, authority, assurance, decision

A skilled payment preparer may be forbidden to release money. That restriction is
not incompetence. A reviewer may authorize a risky exception without making the
underlying evidence stronger. A system may have passed old tests but lack current
assurance after a dependency change. A deployment decision may remain on hold even
when a narrow capability was demonstrated.

Keep all four on the report. Do not let a single badge conceal their differences.
This bundle's machine contract records an assessment-only authority boundary. Real
execution belongs to the host, not to a computed rung.

## Unknown is not a derogatory level

Use `undetermined` when a complete first rung is unsupported. Say which facts are
known, which checks failed, and which remain unknown. A disabled component is not
necessarily incapable; it may simply lack an authorized evaluation environment.
A safe refusal can be evidence of correct operation. Define the task outcome clearly
so restraint is visible rather than treated as lack of initiative.

## Why the fourth rung is not “decentralize everything”

A shared authorization service or central policy owner can be appropriate. The concern
is avoidable routine steering: repeated clarifications, manual retries, format repair,
case-by-case special instructions, and babysitting every consumer. Central infrastructure
can reduce those costs. Distributed workers can increase them.

The choice is architectural, not ideological. Show where decisions are made, where
truth resides, how conflicts are resolved, and who can stop the system. Do not erase
mandatory reviews to improve an autonomy metric.

## Human and biological analogies

The originating conversation used bodies and cells as an analogy for local competence
inside a larger whole. Preserve the useful question—how do specialized parts coordinate?—
without equating molecular regulation with human intention or software authorization.
This skill does not explain consciousness or diagnose human functioning.

For teams, assess role clarity, interfaces, workload, tools, support, and decision rights
with the affected people's input. Never turn the ladder into a ranking of whose life
is worth more, who deserves rest, or whom to humiliate into higher output.
